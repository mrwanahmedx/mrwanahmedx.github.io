## Policy

Projects should not be silently deleted merely because they are weak, old, blocked, or superseded.

Instead:

- keep the artifact when reasonable,
- state its current status,
- point to the maintained replacement,
- preserve failure / blocker history,
- never imply that discontinued work is production-ready.

This status convention is part of the portfolio's engineering history.
